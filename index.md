### Hi, I'm Elijah 👋

I'm a PhD student in the [Cognitive Origins Lab](https://cognitiveoriginslab.psych.wisc.edu/) in the Department of Psychology at the University of Wisconsin-Madison.

I am interested in how different minds (and machines) learn complex things. I currently study how nonhuman primates, children, and adults learn and use computations involved in language and math.
